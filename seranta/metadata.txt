about=Seranta helps FTTH/GPON access-network designers prepare and review designs inside QGIS. Each of its four tools needs only a folder: (1) checks the field survey and prepares the input for automatic design software, (2) estimates FAT and FDT allocation with a link budget, (3) produces post-design code and closure tables, and (4) runs a rule-based QC of the final design package. Every rule reports PASS, ERROR, WARNING, INFO or UNVERIFIED, findings can be browsed on the map, and thresholds are kept in editable profiles. No extra Python packages or internet connection needed. Reports are written in Indonesian. Licence GPL v3 or later with section 7 terms (author attribution kept, modified versions renamed and marked); official releases are digitally signed by the author.

Licence GPL v3 or later with section 7 terms; official releases are digitally signed (Ed25519) and every report states whether the copy is an original signed release. 3.8.2 One combined HTML for a one-PIC/adjacent group (REKAP_GRUP_1PIC_*.html) and for a whole batch (REKAP_BATCH_*.html, also for unrelated clusters): a tab per cluster holding that cluster's full interactive report (map, rule cards, filters, finding pictures), and on the first tab the remarks of every cluster with an extra-remarks box and status choice per cluster (final remarks with 255-character counter, kept in the browser), a group note, every cluster's standards and findings, and one Word download (.docx made in the browser, offline) plus a CSV of the final remarks.
