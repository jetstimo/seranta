about=Seranta helps FTTH/GPON access-network designers prepare and review designs inside QGIS. Each of its four tools needs only a folder: (1) checks the field survey and prepares the input for automatic design software, (2) estimates FAT and FDT allocation with a link budget, (3) produces post-design code and closure tables, and (4) runs a rule-based QC of the final design package. Every rule reports PASS, ERROR, WARNING, INFO or UNVERIFIED, findings can be browsed on the map, and thresholds are kept in editable profiles. No extra Python packages or internet connection needed. Reports are written in Indonesian. Licence GPL v2 or later.
